Support

How to get help

We support every app listed on this website, for Jira Cloud and Confluence Cloud.

  • Support portal: https://jeereemiassb.atlassian.net/servicedesk/customer/portal/2
  • Email: support@jeereemiassb.atlassian.net

What to include

  • The app name and the product (Jira or Confluence).
  • Your site address, for example yourcompany.atlassian.net.
  • What you did, what you expected and what happened, with screenshots if possible.

Please do not send passwords, API tokens or confidential content.

Response times

We aim to answer within 2 business days (Monday to Friday, Central European Time, excluding public holidays in Spain). Problems that stop an app from working for everyone in your site are handled first.

Report a security issue

Send the details to support@jeereemiassb.atlassian.net with "Security" in the subject. We confirm receipt within 2 business days and fix vulnerabilities within the deadlines of Atlassian's security bug fix policy. Please do not disclose the issue publicly until it is fixed.
