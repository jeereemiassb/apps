Inline Tooltips for Confluence: Hover Definitions

Explain terms right in the text: readers hover to see the definition.

Overview

Add a short explanation to any word or phrase of a page. Readers hover over it to read the definition without leaving the text, so acronyms, codes and jargon stop getting in the way.

  • An inline Tooltip macro that sits inside a sentence, a list or a table cell.
  • Dotted underline, an information icon, or both.
  • The text and the explanation are saved in the page itself.

Getting started

  1. A Confluence administrator installs the app from its Atlassian Marketplace listing.
  2. Edit a page, place the cursor where the term goes, type /Tooltip and insert the macro.
  3. In the settings, write the Text on the page and the Tooltip, and choose the Style.
  4. Publish. Readers hover over the text to see the explanation.

Macro settings

SettingWhat it does
Text on the pageThe word or phrase shown in the text.
TooltipThe explanation shown on hover, up to 1,000 characters.
StyleDotted underline (default), info icon, or both.

Limitations

  • The explanation is plain text.
  • Anyone who can read the page can read its tooltips.
  • Without an active license, the text is shown without the tooltip.

Permissions

The app asks for no Confluence permissions.

Data and privacy

The app stores nothing and has no backend: the text and the explanation are saved in the page as macro settings. No data leaves Atlassian. See the privacy policy.

Troubleshooting

The app says it needs an active license

The app is paid and checks the license on every request. Ask your site administrator to check the app's subscription in the administration of Jira or Confluence (Apps → Manage apps). During a free trial the app works normally.
