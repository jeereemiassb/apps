Group Mentions for Jira

Mention a whole group in a comment: every member who can see the issue gets notified.

Overview

Mention a whole group in an issue comment. Type the group's handle after the prefix, for example @@dev-team, and the app replies right below your comment with a mention of every member of that group who can see the issue. Each of them gets Jira's usual mention notification.

  • Works in comments on any issue, in Jira and Jira Service Management.
  • Only groups chosen by a Jira administrator can be mentioned, each with its own handle.
  • Members who cannot see the issue are left out, and nobody is mentioned twice.
  • The reply keeps the visibility of your comment: restricted to a role or group, or internal in Jira Service Management.

Getting started

  1. A Jira administrator installs the app from its Atlassian Marketplace listing.
  2. Go to Jira settings → Apps → Group mentions.
  3. Under Find a group, search for a group and select Add. Its handle is filled in from the group name; change it if you like.
  4. Select Save.
  5. In any issue, add a comment with the prefix and the handle, for example @@dev-team please review.

Settings

SettingWhat it does
Groups people can mentionThe groups that can be mentioned and the handle people type for each one. Handles use lowercase letters, numbers, dots, hyphens and underscores.
PrefixWhat people type before the handle. The default is @@. It can be 1 to 5 symbols, with no letters, numbers or spaces.
Largest group (members)Groups with more members than this are not notified; the reply says so. From 1 to 100; the default is 50.
Also add the people mentioned as watchersWhen on, the people mentioned also become watchers of the issue.

Handles are not case sensitive: @@Dev-Team and @@dev-team are the same. A handle inside code (inline code or a code block) is ignored.

Limitations

  • Only new comments are read. Editing a comment to add a handle does not notify anyone.
  • Groups with more members than the limit are not notified.
  • Inactive users and app accounts are never mentioned. The person who wrote the comment is not mentioned.
  • The reply is posted by the app, so it appears as a comment from Group Mentions.

Permissions

ScopeWhy
storage:appSave the settings.
read:jira-workRead the comment that mentions the group, and its visibility.
write:jira-workPost the reply with the mentions, and add watchers if that option is on.
read:jira-userRead the members of the allowed groups, search groups on the settings page, and check who can see the issue.

No egress: the app does not connect to any server outside Atlassian.

Data and privacy

The app stores only its settings: the allowed groups, their handles and the limits. It does not store account IDs or comment text. When a comment mentions an allowed group, the app reads the group's members and posts a reply inside Jira. Nothing is logged except error codes and no data leaves Atlassian. See the privacy policy.

Troubleshooting

Nothing happens when I type the handle

Check that the group is in the list on the Group mentions page, that the handle matches, and that the prefix is right before it (for example @@dev-team, not @@ dev-team). The prefix must not be glued to a word: name@@dev-team is ignored.

The reply says the group was not notified

The group has more members than the limit set on the Group mentions page. Raise the limit or mention a smaller group.

Some members were not mentioned

They cannot see the issue, they are inactive, they wrote the comment or they were already mentioned in it.

The app says it needs an active license

The app is paid and checks the license on every request. Ask your site administrator to check the app's subscription in Jira administration (Apps → Manage apps). During a free trial the app works normally.
