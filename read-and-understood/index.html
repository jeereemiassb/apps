Overview

Ask people and groups to confirm that they have read and understood a Confluence page, such as a policy or a procedure, and keep a record of who confirmed which version and when.

  • Required readers: people, groups or both.
  • Confirm again after each new version, or once is enough.
  • Confirm from the page header or from a signature box inside the page.
  • Page report, space report and a personal list of pending reads.

Set up a page

  1. A Confluence administrator installs the app from its Atlassian Marketplace listing.
  2. Open the page and, in the page header under the title, select Read & understood, then the Settings tab. Only people who can edit the page can change it.
  3. Turn on Require read confirmation on this page.
  4. Add Required readers (people) and/or Required readers (groups).
  5. Under When the page changes, choose Ask everyone to confirm again after each new version or One confirmation is enough.
  6. Select Save.

Optional: edit the page, type / and insert the Read & understood signature box macro, so readers can confirm inside the page.

Confirming a page

Readers open Read & understood in the page header, tab My confirmation, or use the signature box, and select I have read and understood this page (version N).

If the page requires a new confirmation after each version and it has changed since you confirmed, you are asked to read it again.

Moving from another app

Confluence administrators can open Read & understood from the Confluence settings (⚙). Enter a space key and select Scan space to find the confirmation records that a previous read-and-understood app left in Confluence. Importing those records into this app is planned for a future version.

Limitations

  • Confirmations are recorded per page version. Confluence keeps the page history; the app records which version each person confirmed.
  • Group members are resolved when the report is built, so people who join a required group later appear as pending.

Data and privacy

For each page: the settings (required account IDs and group IDs, and the rule). For each confirmation: the Atlassian account ID, the page version and the date. Page content is never stored. Everything is kept in Atlassian's Forge storage; no data leaves Atlassian. See the privacy policy.

Troubleshooting

I do not see the Settings tab

Only people who can edit the page can configure it.

Someone confirmed but still appears as outdated

The page changed after they confirmed and the rule asks for a new confirmation after each version.

The app says it needs an active license

The app is paid and checks the license on every request. Ask your site administrator to check the app's subscription in the administration of Jira or Confluence (Apps → Manage apps). During a free trial the app works normally.
