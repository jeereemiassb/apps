Watchers+: Group & Label Watches for Confluence

Add a whole group as watchers of a page, page tree or space, follow labels and clean up watches.

Overview

Confluence adds watchers one person at a time, has no way to follow a label, and makes you stop watching pages one by one. Watchers+ adds what is missing, using Confluence's own watches, so people get the notifications they already know:

  • Groups and people as watchers. Space admins add every member of a group, or chosen people, as watchers of a page, a page and all its child pages, a blog post or the whole space. They can also remove them again.
  • Current watchers. Space admins see who watches a page, a blog post or the space, and remove people who no longer need the notifications.
  • Label subscriptions. Anyone can subscribe to labels, in all spaces or only in some. When a page or blog post gets one of those labels, the app makes the subscriber a watcher of it.
  • My watches. Each person sees the pages and blog posts they watch and stops watching the selected ones, or all of them, in one go. The same page finds the spaces they watch.
  • Watch a page tree yourself. Start or stop watching a page and all its child pages with one click.

The app never gives anyone more rights than Confluence does: changing other people's watches is limited to space admins and Confluence admins, and nobody is made a watcher of content they cannot see. Runs on Atlassian: no data leaves Atlassian's cloud.

Getting started

  1. A Confluence administrator installs the app from its Atlassian Marketplace listing.
  2. Open any page or blog post, open the More actions (•••) menu at the top and select Watchers+.
  3. The first time, Confluence asks you to allow the app to act on your behalf. Select Allow access: from then on the app does exactly what you could do yourself in Confluence, with your permissions.
  4. For your own watches and label subscriptions, open My watches from the Apps menu in the top navigation.
  5. Space admins find Space watchers in Space settings → Integrations.

Add a group or people as watchers

Only space admins of that space and Confluence admins can do this. It is Confluence's own rule for changing other people's watches, and the app asks Confluence before it starts.

  1. On the page or blog post, select ••• → Watchers+ → Groups and people.
  2. On a page, turn on Include all child pages to apply it to the whole page tree (up to 2,000 pages).
  3. Choose one or more groups, and people if you like.
  4. Select Add as watchers. A progress bar shows how far it got, and the message at the end says how many watches were added and how many failed.

Remove as watchers does the opposite for the same groups and people. Each person then gets Confluence's usual notifications about that content, according to their own notification settings.

Only the people who are members at that moment are added. Someone who joins the group later is not added automatically: run it again to include them. App accounts in a group are skipped.

See and remove current watchers

  • A page or blog post: ••• → Watchers+ → Current watchers lists everyone who watches it. Select people and Remove selected (space admins and Confluence admins).
  • A space: Space settings → Integrations → Space watchers lists everyone who watches the space. From there you can add groups or people as space watchers, remove them again, or remove selected watchers. People who watch a space get Confluence's notifications about the pages and blog posts in it.

Label subscriptions

  1. Open My watches → Label subscriptions.
  2. Write a label (for example release-notes) and, optionally, a space key to follow it only in that space. Select Subscribe. Add the same label again with another space key to follow it in several spaces; subscribing without a space key follows it everywhere.
  3. From then on, when a page or blog post you can see gets that label, or is created or copied with it, the app makes you a watcher of it within a few seconds. You then get Confluence's notifications about its edits and comments.
  4. Watch existing content makes you a watcher of what already has the label (up to 2,000 items).
  5. Recently created with your labels lists the newest pages and blog posts with your labels.

Unsubscribe stops new watches; the pages you already watch stay watched (use Pages and blog posts to clean them up). Each person can follow up to 50 labels, each in up to 20 spaces or in all of them. Only global labels count, not personal ones.

Clean up your watches

My watches → Pages and blog posts lists everything you watch, most recently changed first.

  • Filter by type (pages or blog posts) or by space key and select Apply.
  • Select items and Stop watching selected, or Select all shown.
  • Stop watching all that match asks for confirmation and then stops your watches on every page and blog post that matches the filter (up to 5,000 per run).

My watches → Spaces → Find the spaces I watch checks each space you can see and lists the ones you watch, so you can stop watching one or all of them. Confluence has no direct list of watched spaces, so on sites with many spaces this takes a little while.

Watchers+ → For me on a page starts or stops your own watch on the page, or on the page and all its child pages.

Limitations

  • Current members only. Adding a group adds its members at that moment. People who join the group later are not added; run it again. Groups are read up to 5,000 members, and one operation can change up to 20,000 watches (pages × people). For more, add the group as space watchers.
  • Label subscriptions make you a watcher; they do not send a separate notification. Confluence does not let apps send their own notifications, so you are not told the moment a page gets the label. You are notified about its later edits and comments, and you can see new labelled content in Recently created with your labels.
  • Up to 200 subscribers per label event. If more than 200 people follow the same label, the rest are not added for that page. Only content that the subscriber can see is added; drafts are skipped.
  • No blog-only space watch. Confluence Cloud's API has no separate "watch only the blog of a space", so Watchers+ manages space watches as a whole, plus the watchers of each blog post.
  • Search delay. The list of pages you watch comes from Confluence search, which can take a minute to reflect changes. The app removes items from the list as soon as you stop watching them.
  • Lists show the first 1,000 watchers of a page or space, and the group picker shows the first 1,000 groups of the site.
  • The page tree includes child pages only (not whiteboards, databases or folders), up to 2,000 pages.

No egress: the app does not connect to any server outside Atlassian.

Data and privacy

The app stores only label subscriptions: the Atlassian account ID of the person, the label and the space keys they chose. Watches themselves are stored by Confluence, as usual. The app does not store names, email addresses, group memberships or page content.

When you use the app it reads, as you and with your permissions, the pages of a tree, the members of the groups you choose, the watchers of a page or space and the content you watch. When a page gets a label, the app reads its labels, the subscriptions for those labels and whether each subscriber can see the page. It uses that information only while it works and does not keep it. Because the app stores account IDs, it reports them to Atlassian every week through the personal data reporting API and deletes the subscriptions of accounts that were closed. Everything stays in Atlassian's cloud (Forge storage follows your site's data residency). See the privacy policy.

Troubleshooting

"Only space admins and Confluence admins can change other people's watches"

That is Confluence's rule for adding or removing other people as watchers. Ask a space admin of that space to do it. You can always change your own watches with For me and My watches.

Some watches failed

The final message counts failures. Common reasons: the person's account is deactivated, the person cannot see that page, or the page was deleted meanwhile. Run the operation again to retry; watches that already exist are not duplicated.

I subscribed to a label but a page was not added

  • The label must be added to a page or blog post you can see; drafts are skipped until they are published.
  • Personal labels (my:) do not count.
  • If you limited the subscription to some spaces, the page must be in one of them.
  • It takes a few seconds, because Confluence delivers label events to apps asynchronously.
  • If you added the label yourself, the app leaves your watch as it is.

A page I stopped watching still appears in My watches

Confluence search takes a moment to update. Select Apply again after a minute.

"This app needs an active license"

The app only works with an active subscription or trial. Check the app in Confluence administration → Apps → Manage apps.
