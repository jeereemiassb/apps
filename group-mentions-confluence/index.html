Group Mentions for Confluence

Mention a whole group in a comment: every member who can see the page gets notified.

Overview

Confluence mentions reach one person at a time. With Group Mentions, you type @@ and a group handle in a comment, for example @@dev-team, and the app replies to your comment with a mention of each member of that group. Because they are normal Confluence mentions, each person is notified the way they already are for any mention (in Confluence and by email, according to their own notification settings).

  • Works in comments at the bottom of a page or blog post and in inline comments.
  • Only members who can see the page are mentioned. The comment author and people already mentioned in the comment are left out.
  • Confluence administrators choose which groups can be mentioned, the handle of each group, a member limit and the prefix.
  • The page itself is never edited.
  • Runs on Atlassian: no data leaves Atlassian's cloud.

Getting started

  1. A Confluence administrator installs the app from its Atlassian Marketplace listing.
  2. Go to Confluence administration → Settings → Apps → Group mentions. (Only Confluence administrators see this page.)
  3. Under Add a group, choose a group and select Add. The app suggests a handle based on the group name (lowercase, with hyphens instead of spaces, for example "Dev Team" becomes dev-team). You can change it.
  4. Repeat for every group people should be able to mention. Groups that are not in this list cannot be mentioned.
  5. Select Save.

Now anyone who can comment on a page can write @@dev-team in a comment.

Using group mentions

  1. Open a page or blog post and add a comment, at the bottom of the page or inline on selected text.
  2. Write the prefix and the handle anywhere in the comment, for example: Rollout steps are ready. @@dev-team please review. Handles are not case sensitive, so @@Dev-Team works too. You can mention several groups in the same comment.
  3. Post the comment. A few seconds later, the app replies to it:
    • Dev Team: @Ana @Bea @Carl: each person mentioned gets a notification.
    • Dev Team was not notified: it has more than 50 members.: the group is larger than the member limit.
    • Nobody in Dev Team was notified: no other member can see this page.
    • Dev Team has no other members to notify.: everyone in the group is the author or was already mentioned.

If some members cannot see the page, the reply says how many were not notified, without naming them.

A handle is ignored when it is inside code (inline code or a code block), when it is attached to a word (as in an email address like team@@dev), or when the group is not in the administrator's list.

Editing a comment. If the author edits their own comment within 7 days and adds another group, the app replies again for the new group. A group is notified only once per comment, however many times the comment is edited.

The reply shows the members it mentions to everyone who can see the page, so it reveals who belongs to the group. Only add groups whose membership can be visible to the people who read your pages.

Limitations

  • The mentions are in a reply from the app. Confluence does not let apps add notifications to someone else's comment, so the app posts a reply with the mentions. The reply appears as a comment by the app. People who watch the page also get the usual notification about a new comment.
  • Comments only. Mentions in the page body are not processed, because editing the page would notify its watchers again. Comments on attachments, whiteboards, databases and other content types are not processed.
  • Up to 5 groups per comment and up to 100 members per group. Extra groups in the same comment are listed as not notified.
  • Edits count only when the comment author makes them, within 7 days of posting the comment.
  • The reply takes a few seconds, because Confluence delivers comment events to apps asynchronously.
  • The group list on the settings page shows the first 1,000 groups of the site.
  • People are mentioned only if the app can confirm that they can see the page. If Confluence does not answer that check, they are not mentioned and the reply says so.

No egress: the app does not connect to any server outside Atlassian.

Data and privacy

The app stores its settings (the IDs and names of the groups that can be mentioned, their handles, the prefix and the member limit) and, for 8 days, the IDs of the comments it has answered with the handles of the groups it notified. It stores no account IDs, names or comment text.

When a comment mentions a group, the app reads the comment, the members of the group and whether each of them can see the page. It uses that information only while it prepares the reply and does not keep it. The reply is a normal Confluence comment, stored by Confluence. Everything stays in Atlassian's cloud (Forge storage follows your site's data residency). See the privacy policy.

Troubleshooting

Nothing happens after I write @@handle

  • Check that the group is in Confluence administration → Settings → Apps → Group mentions and that the handle and prefix match.
  • The handle must not be inside code formatting or attached to a word.
  • Wait a few seconds and reload the page: the reply is posted asynchronously.
  • The comment must be on a page or blog post.
  • The app needs an active license (see below).

The reply says "it has more than N members"

The group is larger than the member limit. An administrator can raise the limit (up to 100), or you can mention a smaller group.

Someone in the group was not notified

They may not be able to see the page (restricted page or space permissions), they may be the author of the comment, or they were already mentioned in it. Mention notifications also follow each person's own Confluence notification settings.

The reply says "the app could not check who can see this page"

Confluence did not answer the permission check. Nobody is mentioned in that case, so no one who cannot see the page is notified. Post the comment again later and, if it keeps happening, contact support.

"This app needs an active license"

The settings page and the group mentions only work with an active subscription or trial. Check the app in Confluence administration → Apps → Manage apps.
